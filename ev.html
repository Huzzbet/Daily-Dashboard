What counts as value?

BetIQ first removes the vig at each bookmaker, averages the resulting no-vig probability across books, then compares each book's price with that market consensus. A minimum of 3 books is required by default. This is a market-based EV estimate, not a prediction model. If a future model probability is supplied in the feed, the scanner also shows model EV separately.

How the engine works

1. Group identical markets and lines across bookmakers. 2. For each book that quotes both sides, remove the vig by normalising implied probabilities. 3. Average those no-vig probabilities to get the market consensus. 4. Compare each book's payout against the field and apply a 20% best-price penalty. 5. Z-score the composite within market pools to create the BetIQ Score (mean 100, SD 15). 6. Calculate EV and Kelly from the consensus probability.
