Methodology

For each identical market and line, BetIQ removes vig at each bookmaker that quotes both sides, averages those no-vig probabilities to form market consensus, compares each book's price with the field, applies a 20% best-price penalty, then normalises the composite to a Score with mean 100 and SD 15. EV and Kelly use market consensus. This mirrors the published myBetIQ methodology.
